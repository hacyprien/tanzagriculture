Agriculture in Tanzania

Growing food, livelihoods, and opportunity

Tanzania’s agriculture is a foundation of rural life, national food security, and export earnings. From fertile highlands to wide plains, farming shapes the future of many communities.

Explore the story

Major agricultural areas

Morogoro

Known for rice, maize, and horticultural production, Morogoro supports many smallholder farmers and agro-processing activities.

Arusha and Kilimanjaro

These regions are important for coffee, tea, vegetables, and fruit, especially in cooler highland areas.

Mbeya

Mbeya is a major hub for tea, coffee, and food crops, helping sustain rural incomes and local markets.

Shinyanga and Rukwa

These areas are important for grain production, livestock, and mixed farming systems that support many households.

Impact on communities

Why agriculture matters

  • Creates jobs for millions of families in rural areas.
  • Provides food, income, and stability for local communities.
  • Supports schools, health services, and small businesses.
  • Helps Tanzania earn income through export crops.

“Agriculture is not only about food; it is about dignity, growth, and the future of villages across Tanzania.”
